Quana Cannon — Artist Biography

Biography & Studio Stack

Quana Cannon (b. 2004) is an interdisciplinary multimedia artist working across fine art, ceramic sculpture, lens media, and dance performance.

Holding an MFA from UdK Berlin and B.S. from RISD in Fine Art & Ceramics, Rivers explores tactile clay acoustics, glaze chemistry, and human kinesphere movement notation.

Exhibitions include Venice Biennale, Tate Modern London, Shigaraki Ceramic Cultural Park Japan, and Mori Art Museum Tokyo.
