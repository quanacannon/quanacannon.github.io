Alex Rivers — Artist Profile

Biography & Practice

Alex Rivers (b. 1994) is a London and Berlin-based multimedia artist working at the intersection of e-textiles, real-time GLSL fragment shaders, spatial acoustics, and tactile installation art.

Holding an MFA in Computational Arts from UdK Berlin and B.S. in Electrical Engineering from MIT, Rivers crafts tactile physical garments embedded with Velostat pressure sensors, alongside zero-gravity raytraced visual geometry.

Their work has been presented internationally at Venice Biennale, Mori Art Center Tokyo, ZKM Center for Art and Media, and Ars Electronica Linz.
